CONSTRAINTS FOR ASSIGNED ROBOT LINES (VERY IMPORTANT):

1. The line MUST be in exactly one of these forms:
   - **Assigned Robot**: robotN

   - No spaces inside: use "robot1", NOT "Robot 1" or "robot 1".
   - Do NOT spell numbers as words ("robot one", "robot two") – always digits.
   - Do NOT add any extra punctuation or question marks around the name
     (NO "? robot1 ?", NO "robot1?").

   - "robots", "any robot", "a robot", "team", "group", "any available robot",
